Dear Sophia,

A few days ago, a cow delivered a very official letter summoning you to a magical weekend in Music City, Nashville. It turns out the magic was real.

We picked out our very first cowboy hats, watched a full orchestra bring Harry Potter to life, sang KTV with new friends, sipped some seriously good matcha, and learned a few ballroom steps in West End Tower. Then, the very next day, the universe sent us a whole crowd dancing salsa by the river, and we danced too.

I planned some of it down to the minute. My favorite parts, I didn't plan at all.

Thank you for coming all this way.

Yours, big time,
Toto
